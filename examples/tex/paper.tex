\section{Energy}
Einstein's familiar equation relates energy $E$, mass $m$, and the speed of
light $c$:
\begin{equation}
E = mc^2.
\label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}

\section{Integration}
The power rule gives
\begin{equation}
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac{1}{3}.
\label{eq:integral}
\end{equation}
Equations~\eqref{eq:energy} and~\eqref{eq:integral} show numbered equations
and cross-references in a compact document.

\section{A matrix}
The determinant of this matrix is $-2$:
\[
A = \begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det(A) = 1\cdot4 - 2\cdot3 = -2.
\]

\section{A worked calculation}
Integrating a polynomial term by term gives
\begin{align*}
\int_0^1 (3x^2 + 2x + 1)\,dx
&= \left[x^3 + x^2 + x\right]_0^1 \\
&= 1 + 1 + 1 \\
&= 3.
\end{align*}
For comparison, the power rule states
\[
\int_0^1 x^n\,dx = \frac{1}{n+1}, \qquad n > -1.
\]
