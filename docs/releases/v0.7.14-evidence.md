# v0.7.14

Landing record for annotated release `v0.7.14`. The final release commit is the object resolved by `v0.7.14^{commit}`; the exact pushed SHA is recorded in the release handoff after the tag and origin refs are verified.

## Shipped scope

- Local-service lease refreshes retain their existing lease identity instead of minting one row per refresh until a healthy daemon refuses new clients and stays pinned awake.
- Process-claim adoption is a compare-and-swap over the recorded supervisor and adoption counter. A contending successor loses without signalling, unreadable identities fail closed, namespace checks compare the persisted namespace against the resolved service root, and a newly started service runs the bounded repair owner for strictly older claimed survivors. One daemon decision now scopes adoption to that daemon's exact PID, so another adoptable claim in the shared ledger cannot change supervisors as a side effect.
- Orphan repair re-proves the persisted process-start identity throughout the SIGTERM-to-SIGKILL escalation. If the PID is reused after the graceful signal, repair refuses the force signal, retains the claim, and reports the typed identity-reuse reason.
- watchd publishes an explicit failed generation and keeps idle and pending-generation states distinct from a scan failure. A running daemon with no registered scope is healthy-idle, an unactivated generation is starting, and only the exact failed generation contributes a degraded backend-health result.
- YO!stats paints accepted history while focus remains on graph-shell controls. Snapshot source-generation floors are owned per exact range and resolution, so an accepted selection cannot move backward while a newly selected range may legitimately begin from an older materialization.
- The release retains complete gate diagnostics, shares listener-census and filesystem-batch test owners, removes certification negative-control load before measured units, and waits for jobd scheduler initialization through its lifecycle owner.
- The statsd ring invalidation, exact tmux target, and macOS launch-environment records that were completed before this boundary are indexed in the repository's DONE archive. The broader backend-lifetime, filesystem-delete, memory, and descriptor queues remain open where their own unchecked requirements say they remain open; this release record does not close them.
